📶 Offline & sync

No signal on site? Keep working — time in/out, selfies, GPS, manual entries and edits are saved on this phone and upload automatically when internet returns (checked every 20 s). Open the app once with internet so it can work offline after that.
How it works
• Kiosk: worker taps name → enters 4-digit PIN → takes selfie → Time In / Time Out.
• Late = time-in after shift start + grace. Minutes counted from shift start.
• Hours = out − in, minus lunch if worked > 5h. OT = hours beyond regular.
• Est. pay = (regular hrs ÷ reg/day × daily rate) + OT hrs × hourly × OT multiplier.
• Staff, Reports & Settings are locked behind the Admin PIN (default 1234).
